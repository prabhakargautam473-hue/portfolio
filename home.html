About me

My name is Chandrakar Parsad Gautam, and I am an aspiring data analyst. My interest in data began with a simple question: how do organisations decide what to do next? I soon realised that behind almost every good decision there is data that someone has taken the time to understand.

That curiosity led me to learn data analytics from the ground up. I am building my skills in Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI, and I practise on real datasets so that what I learn is not just theory but something I can apply.

I approach every project with patience and attention to detail. I would rather check a number twice than present a wrong conclusion, and I enjoy turning a messy spreadsheet into a clear answer that anyone can understand.

My process is simple. I start by understanding the question, then I clean the data, look for patterns, and finally present the findings in a way that a non-technical person can use. I believe an analysis is only useful when someone can act on it.

Right now I am practising Excel for data cleaning and pivot tables, SQL for querying databases, Python for deeper analysis, and Power BI for dashboards. Each tool teaches me something new, and I try to build a small project with everything I learn.

Beyond the technical side, I value honesty with data. I would rather say “the data does not show this” than force a conclusion. I also like explaining my work in simple words, because good analysis should be understood by everyone, not only by analysts.

I am always open to learning, feedback and new opportunities.
